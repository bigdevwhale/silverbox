# Changelog

## [1.1.0]

- Reminders to take a picture, 1 to 5 a day (or off) inside a window you choose, each with something worth a frame and a "Shoot" button that opens the camera. Quiet if you shot something in the last hour and a half.
- The projector is softer and quieter, and stays off once muted.

## [1.0.0]

- First release: rolls of film shot with the phone's own camera, the lab (a day, or an hour once a week), envelopes of prints, the album, the shoebox and the shelf with contact sheets and negatives.
- Five photo films and two 8 mm cassettes; prints developed on the phone with grain, halation, light leaks, the cut last frame, expired film and the orange date stamp.
- 8 mm home movies cut by the lab: three seconds a clip, 18 fps, intertitles, dust and flicker, with a projector to watch them on.
- Writing on the backs of prints, "on this day", a shot list for each roll, backup to one zip.
- English and Russian.
